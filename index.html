Model card — how this was built
Development population1,251 adults, 688 deaths, 4 independent cohorts
OutcomeOverall survival from diagnosis, not censored at transplant
ModelRidge-penalized Cox; age and log₂(WBC) as restricted cubic splines; three pre-specified interactions
Validation1,000 bootstrap replicates and leave-one-cohort-out, with the entire pipeline — including selection of the penalty parameter — re-run inside every resample and fold
CalibrationNo additional uniform shrinkage was applied — the ridge penalty already performs it. Recalibration slope in the development population 1.02; mean absolute error 3 percentage points.

Limitations
  • No external validation in a locked cohort — the four cohorts contributed to development.
  • Absolute risk should be re-calibrated before use in a new population.
  • Development cohorts predate widespread use of FLT3 inhibitors and venetoclax.
  • Adverse cytogenetics is not part of the model (17 patients in development); this tool does not cover that patient.
  • Observational data: effects are adjusted, not causal.
  • The model assumes proportional hazards. This assumption is mildly violated (Schoenfeld global p = 0.03), which is the likely reason estimates are least accurate in the best-prognosis group.
Research tool. Intended to support clinical discussion and study design. It does not replace clinical judgement and is not a treatment recommendation.
